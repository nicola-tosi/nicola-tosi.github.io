I am a research scientist at the [Institute of Space Research](https://www.dlr.de/en/wr/) of the [German Aerospace Center (DLR)](https://www.dlr.de) in Berlin. My research focuses on the interior structure and on the thermal and compositional evolution of solar system planets and exoplanets. To this end, I use a variety of numerical models to simulate the dynamics and evolution of planetary interiors and interpret observations made by space missions. I am also interested in applying machine learning techniques in the frame of surrogate forward modelling of interior dynamics and of inverse modelling of planetary interior structure and dynamics to rapidly infer model parameters from observations.  

I am an [editor](https://planetary-research.org/editorial-board) of the Diamond Open Access Journal [Planetary Research](https://planetary-research.org/), the first community-led journal devoted to the broad field of planetary science where all articles are free to access and are published at no cost for authors.
